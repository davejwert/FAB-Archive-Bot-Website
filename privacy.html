FAB Archive Bot Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

1. Overview

FAB Archive Bot is a private Discord integration used to archive and organize communications from authorized Discord channels.

2. Information Collected

Depending on the authorized channel and available Discord permissions, the application may process:

  • Discord usernames and user IDs
  • Message content and message IDs
  • Message timestamps
  • Message edits and deletion events
  • Attachment information and files
  • Channel identifiers and message references

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed for authorized communication archiving, historical reference, operational review, and potential translation and summarization features.

4. Storage and Access

Archived information may be stored in private application storage and authorized Google Drive storage. Access is intended to be limited to authorized administrators and personnel.

5. Google API Access

The application requests Google Drive authorization to create and manage archive files associated with its operation. Google account authorization is used for this purpose and not for advertising.

6. Information Sharing

Archived communications are not made publicly available through this website. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application, including Discord, Google, and the application hosting provider.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Archived information is retained for authorized corporation recordkeeping purposes. Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion may be directed to the application administrator. Requests are reviewed subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.

8. Security

The application uses restricted access and private storage where configured. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when application functionality or data handling practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted to the application administrator through the project's public GitHub repository issue tracker. Do not include private Discord messages or sensitive information in public issues.
