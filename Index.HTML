About FAB Archive Bot

FAB Archive Bot is a private Discord integration designed to help authorized corporation members preserve and organize communications.

Purpose

The application supports the archiving of authorized Discord channel messages, including message history, timestamps, and attachment information.

Archived information may be used for communication review, translation, and operational summaries.

Data Security

Archived communications are not published on this website. Access to stored information is restricted to authorized personnel.
